Motivation

Why price the defense?

  • Banking assistants expose account tools → natural target for jailbreaks, prompt injection, fraud help
  • AI-enabled data breaches average US$ 4.99 M; financial sector US$ 6.3 M; 20%+ of orgs report AI-related breaches (IBM, 2026)
  • Small guard models (22 M–4 B params) promise a cheap input filter — but self-reported scores diverge across benchmarks
  • The expensive piece is the LLM judge: should it see every message, or only the hard ones?

Guard model

86 M-class encoder ≈ cents per call — but recall varies by benchmark and language

LLM judge

Strong, language-agnostic — priced per token, on every single message

Our question

What does the whole pipeline cost per million messages?

Slide 6 · Cost × recall — do not cut1:10
Sensitivity analysis

The cascade wins when attacks are rare

Sensitivity vs legitimate clients blocked per month (15 M msgs, π=1%); C1 (L4) routes eligible to the SLA highlighted.
  • Cheapest-route map: cascade wins 13/24 EN cells at π=0.1% — 7/24 at π=1%, 4/24 at π=5%
  • Above ~1% prevalence, cheap guard-only takes most cells
  • PT: escalation volume breaks the 300 ms SLA — winner is guard-only, US$ 196,733/1M
  • L4 → H100 moves cost only +0.4%: defense is judge-API dominated
  • Threshold choice unstable across bootstraps (test sens. 0.75–0.95): the winner is not uniquely optimal
Slide 7 · Sensitivity · compact 0:250:50
Limitations — the honest slide

What these numbers do not show

  • External jailbreak recall is low: FinVault overall 18%, jailbreak subset 8.6% — a harder attack regime than our corpus
  • L4 p95 is extrapolated (52–1,742 ms range), not measured on real L4 hardware
  • Synthetic financial attacks (LLM-generated D4) and machine-translated PT corpus
  • π and L are scenario parameters, not measurements — the map is a budgeting tool, not a forecast
  • Judge family bias checked: no self-preference detected, but CIs are wide (n=69/199)
Slide 8 · Limitations0:40
Conclusion

Defend cheap where you can, escalate rarely, price the escalation

  • Cascade is the cheapest SLA-eligible defense at low prevalence (13/24 cells at π=0.1%); guard-only wins when attacks are common
  • Confidence escalation cuts judge traffic to ≈1% at π=1% — savings are measured, not assumed
  • Budget advice: hardware is not the lever (+0.4% L4→H100) — optimize judge pricing and routing first
  • Code, datasets and prompts released for reproducibility
